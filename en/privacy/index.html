How this website works and what happens when you contact us.

The project brief form

The form runs in your browser. It does not send entries to a website database or save them in browser local storage. “Download brief” creates a text file on your device. “Prepare email” passes the entered details to your email application, where you choose whether to send them.

Email inquiries

If you send an inquiry, its contents are received by the business mailbox displayed on the contact page. Include only the information needed to discuss your project. Before sharing confidential assets, agree on appropriate confidentiality terms and a transfer method with the team.

Hosting and external resources

The hosting platform may process technical request information and, for a private preview, authentication information. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the network request needed to supply them. This website does not include added advertising or analytics scripts.

Questions and requests

For questions about information you have shared, contact info@penguinsgroup.com.vn. Please identify the relevant inquiry so the team can review your request.
