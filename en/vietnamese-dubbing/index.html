What we can help with

  • Vietnamese script editing and dialogue adaptation
  • Voice selection and sample approval before production
  • Recording for short drama, narration, and branded video

  • Synchronization and mixing to the agreed specification

Before we get started

Can you deliver a dubbed and subtitled video?

Yes, this can be scoped as a combined service. Provide your subtitle files, source video, and audio delivery requirements so that the full workflow can be confirmed.

How do you quote dubbing projects?

Pricing depends on approved video duration, voice count, script adaptation, synchronization, audio assets, and retake requirements. A sample helps establish the production scope and schedule.
